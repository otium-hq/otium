# Ubiquitous Language

Project-scoped vocabulary. These terms come from otium.run copy, so the words we
build with are the words we sell with. Code, tests, prompts, and ADRs use them
and no synonyms.

| Term | Meaning | Site |
|---|---|---|
| **Rules** | The user's charter — what's worth their time, what isn't, what to handle, what to keep for themselves. Markdown they own. | "You write your own rules." |
| **Demand** | Anything that wants a piece of the user's time: a charge, an email, a call, an errand. | "Every demand on your time" |
| **Sort** | Matching a Demand against the Rules to reach an Outcome. | "gets sorted against it" |
| **Outcome** | The result of a Sort. Exactly three exist. | — |
| **Handled** | Done quietly, without the user. | "handled quietly" |
| **Brought** | Surfaced to the user for a decision. | "brought to you" |
| **Pushed back** | Declined on the user's behalf. | "pushed back" |
| **The honest look** | The weekly report of life beside the Rules. Reports, never grades. | "Once a week, an honest look" |
| **The gap** | Distance between what the user said mattered and what they did. | "The gap doesn't announce itself" |
| **Monoapp** | The client. One app, not a suite. | "The AI future monoapp" |
| **Executor** | Whoever carries out a Demand with a physical outcome — person, service, or robot. Swappable. | "bring your own hardware" |
