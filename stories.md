# Stories

## S-001 — Locked out of the car

My Rules say roadside emergencies under $150 are OTIUM's to handle.

**Given** I am standing next to my locked car,
**when** I tell OTIUM I am locked out,
**then** it finds lockout services that cover where I am, gets a price from each,
dispatches the cheapest, and tells me who is coming and when.

Handled when a quote comes back inside the bound. Brought when the cheapest quote
is over it, when no service covers the location, or when no price can be got
without committing to the job. The way back is a cancellation call.
